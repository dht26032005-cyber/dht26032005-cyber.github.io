Privacy Policy

Tong Hop Kenh ES accesses, with the channel owner's permission, read-only YouTube channel data (videos, views, watch time, retention, traffic sources and revenue figures) through the YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and YouTube Reporting API.

The data is written only into a private Google Sheet owned by the channel owner. We do not sell, share or transfer this data to any third party, and we do not use it for advertising. The app cannot modify, upload or delete anything on the YouTube channel.

The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and can delete the Google Sheet to remove all stored data.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
